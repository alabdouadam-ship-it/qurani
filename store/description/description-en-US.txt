Qurani is a complete Qur'an companion: read, listen, memorise, and keep your prayers on time — in one calm, ad-free app.

NEW — SECONDARY EDITION
Read the Qur'an with a tafsir or translation one tap away. An arrow beside every ayah opens its meaning right where you are, without leaving the page or losing your place. Open several at once to compare, and choose which edition appears.

READ
• Arabic scripts: Simple, Uthmani, and colour-coded Tajweed
• I'rab: word-by-word grammatical analysis
• Printed Mushaf pages in three styles, all 604 pages
• Amiri Quran and KFGQPC Hafs fonts, with adjustable text size
• Highlight verses in colours, bookmark pages, and resume where you stopped
• Ten themes, including dark and high-contrast

TAFSIR AND TRANSLATIONS
• Six tafsir books: Al-Muyassar (King Fahd Complex), Al-Jalalayn, Al-Qurtubi, Tanwir al-Miqbas (Ibn Abbas), Al-Waseet, Al-Baghawi
• Translations: English, French, Turkish (Diyanet Vakfı), German (Bubenheim & Elyas)

LISTEN
• Recitations from a choice of reciters, verse by verse or full surah
• Download recitations for offline listening
• Background playback with lock-screen controls

MEMORISE
• Repeat any range of verses, as many times as you need
• Rotate between reciters on each repetition
• Test yourself and follow your progress

PRAYER TIMES AND QIBLA
• Prayer times for your location, with a full month view
• Adhan notifications with your choice of sound and volume
• Qibla compass

DHIKR
• Tasbeeh counter with your own groups
• Wird: a daily dhikr list with reminders

MORE
• Hadith library
• Search in Arabic, English or French. Arabic search ignores diacritics, and any result takes you straight to that verse on the reading screen
• Arrange the home screen to show only what you use
• Interface in Arabic, English and French

OFFLINE AND PRIVATE
The full Qur'an text and the reading experience work with no internet connection. There are no ads and no account to create. Anonymous usage statistics are optional and can be switched off in settings at any time.
